Our Commitment to Your Privacy

This Privacy Policy explains how CodedHouse (“we”, “our”, or “us”) handles information in the mobile game City Bus Jam. We designed the game so everyone can tap, solve puzzles, and enjoy a clean experience with minimal data footprint.

2

Information We Do Not Collect

You do not need an account to play City Bus Jam. CodedHouse does not ask for, collect, or store personal details on external servers:

  • No names, email addresses, passwords, phone numbers, or chat messages are collected inside the app.
  • Your game progress — current level, unlocked cities, coins, stars, garage skins, and sound or vibration settings — is saved strictly on your device and never transmitted to CodedHouse.
3

Safe for Children & Families

City Bus Jam is suitable for players of all ages, including children and families. Because the game requires no registration and collects no personal profile data, children can play without submitting personal details.

In-app advertising is configured strictly for family-safe, general-audience content. If you are a parent or guardian with any privacy question, please contact us anytime at info@codedhouse.com and we will respond promptly.

4

Family-Safe Advertising

To keep City Bus Jam free to download and play, the app uses Google AdMob to display occasional ads (configured for general, all-ages suitability). Google may process standard technical signals automatically to deliver age-appropriate ads and maintain service reliability, governed by Google’s Privacy Policy.

Where regional privacy regulations require consent, an in-app privacy prompt lets you review or update your preference via the Privacy settings option inside the app.

5

Updates to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time as features evolve. Whenever we do, the revised policy will be published on this page with an updated effective date.
